overview

This bot was built for Rat Haven Studios to improve pixel art skills.

It posts the Lospec Daily to a set discord channel every morning at 8 AM EST, giving artists a regular prompt and palette to practice with.

Not affiliated with Lospec.

how it works

Set it up once with a single slash command and the bot posts Lospec's daily pixel art palette and prompt to your channel every morning at 8 AM EST. No ongoing maintenance needed.

Automatic posting can be enabled or disabled at any time without losing your channel configuration, and /getdailydata shows the current daily palette on demand, outside of the scheduled post. Each server controls its own channel and settings independently.
