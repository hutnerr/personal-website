delta t

$ a story puzzle game about stopping an alien invasion in 2 minutes

overview archived

A story puzzle game built for GMTK 2026 (theme: "Countdown"). Built by Rat Haven Studios!

Aliens are invading the world and you have 2 minutes to stop them. Is that enough time to save the world? Go around the map, talk to your fellow townspeople, and pull on the strings of clues to figure out a way to stop the invasion before time runs out.

Forwarning: this game did not really come out the best and is very unserious as well as unpolished. Definitely not the best work we've done, but still might give someone a good laugh.

controls

  • E - interact

  • R - reset

goal

You have 2 minutes to stop the alien invasion. Go around the map and interact with your fellow townspeople to gather clues. Pull on strings, ask questions, and figure out a way to stop the aliens before time runs out.
