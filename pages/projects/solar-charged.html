solar charged

$ a resource management tower defense game where energy is currency and health

overview archived

A tower defense game built with a partner for Kenny Game Jam 2025 (theme: "Power"). Built by Rat Haven Studios!

Energy is both your currency and your health. Place too many towers and you run out of power, and you lose.

View the rankings on the Jam Page.

tech stack

  • Godot 4, GDScript
  • Explicit state machine (5 game states)
  • Weighted weather state transitions

features

  • Energy doubles as your health. Spending it on towers puts you at risk; drain it completely and it's game over
  • A live weather cycle (Rain, Cloudy, Normal, Sunny) shifts your solar recharge rate mid-wave, forcing you to adapt on the fly
  • Five tower types with distinct roles: basic shooter, piercing laser, area damage, slow, and an energy booster that amplifies nearby towers
  • Each tower has two independent upgrade paths with three levels, letting you specialize your build
  • Between-wave shop phases to regroup, adjust your layout, and prepare for the next push

how to play

Manage your power effectively while defending against waves of enemies. Place and upgrade towers strategically, but the more towers you have and the more you upgrade them, the more power they consume. If you run out of power, you lose.

Power recharges through solar energy, which is affected by the weather cycle. Keep an eye on the forecast. To open the upgrade menu, select a placed tower. You can also delete towers this way. Upkeep is the passive cost of having a tower on the field; it increases when the tower is actively shooting.


tower types

  • Orange Tower: Simple shooter
  • Red Tower: Piercing laser
  • Green Tower: Increases the power of towers in its radius
  • Blue Tower: Slows down enemies by a percent
  • Grey Tower: Deals constant damage in its radius
