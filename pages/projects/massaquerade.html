massaquerade

$ a murder mystery game built in 72 hours for Winter MelonJam 2025

overview archived

A branching narrative mystery built in 72 hours for Winter MelonJam 2025 (theme: "Mask"). Built by Rat Haven Studios!

You play as a hired hitman at the masquerade ball. Talk to guests at a masquerade ball to collect clues that unlock new conversation branches, then decide who to strike.

View the rankings on the Jam Page.

tech stack

  • Godot 4, GDScript
  • JSON dialogue trees (per-character, per-mask branching)

links

  • Game Jam Page

features

  • Play through a branching narrative mystery and curated political story.
  • Talk to guests at a masquerade ball to collect clues that unlock new conversation branches you couldn't access before
  • Every clue you gather can reopen dialogue with characters you've already spoken to, rewarding thorough exploration
