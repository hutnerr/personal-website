overview archived

A cyborg roguelike built for Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2 (theme: "Trust No One"). Built by Rat Haven Studios!

You're a cyborg robot fighter struggling to keep up with the technological advances, the only augments you can get come from sketchy dealers. Beware, they don't always tell the truth...

how to play

controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys - move
  • Z / LMB - attack
  • E - interact
  • Space - dodge
  • I - open journal
  • Full controller support

goal

Power up and kill waves of enemies. Between waves, spend what you earned at the shop, but be careful, the shopkeepers cannot be trusted...

You only find out what something really does once you buy it and see its effect in your Journal. Build up your understanding of what everything actually does, and use what your experiences have taught you to make the right decisions.
