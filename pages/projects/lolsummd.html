lolsummd

aggregate League of Legends champion mastery across all your accounts

maintained Python Flask Redis Vercel
Open Live Site View on GitHub
— github stars

overview

League of Legends players who play on multiple accounts have no built-in way to see their total champion mastery across all accounts at once.

LOLSUMMD aggregates mastery data from up to 10 accounts (any region) into a single mastery table. Try it with this example link to see it pre-loaded with accounts.

Note: currently running on a development API key. Some requests may occasionally fail pending production approval from Riot.

features

Multi-account

Add up to 10 accounts from any region in one session.

Combined mastery

Instantly see a combined ranked list of every champion with mastery points pooled across all accounts.

Mastery badges

Mastery level badges (highlighted at levels 5 and 7) let you spot your most-invested champions at a glance.

Shareable links

Share your account list with one click. The link pre-loads the same accounts for anyone who opens it.

Remembered accounts

Accounts are remembered in your browser so you never have to re-enter them on return visits.

Riot ID pasting

Paste SummonerName#TAG directly. The app splits it for you automatically.

Region auto-fill

Region tag auto-fills to the local default if left blank.
